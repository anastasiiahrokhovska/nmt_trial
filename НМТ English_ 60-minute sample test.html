НМТ English: sample test

60:00

32 questions, 60 minutes, one correct answer each. The structure copies the real НМТ foreign-language test (32 tasks). The questions are original practice items, not the official ones: for released real tasks use the online tests at osvita.ua or the УЦОЯО demo. The timer starts with your first answer or the Start button. Explanations and homework unlock after you finish.

Speaking, 5 minutes (extra practice: the real НМТ has no speaking part)

Work with a partner or record yourself on your phone. The parts change automatically.

5:00
Part 1 (1 min): warm-up

Tell me about yourself: where you live and what you study. What do you do in your free time? Which school subject is your favourite, and why?

Part 2 (2 min): monologue

Talk about a place in Ukraine you would recommend to a foreign friend. Say where it is, what people can do there, why you like it, and when the best time to go is.

Part 3 (2 min): discussion

“Teenagers should have a limit on screen time.” Give your opinion, one argument for, one against, and an example. Useful: In my opinion…, On the other hand…, For instance…, That is why…

Self-check (1 = weak, 5 = strong)
